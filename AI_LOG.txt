4. Error Troubleshooting and Debugging

One of the main ways I used AI was for troubleshooting errors.

When I encountered errors that I could not understand or solve independently, I took screenshots of the errors and sent them directly to Claude Code through the regular chat interface.

The process included:

Encountering an error while working in Unity or during project development.

Taking a screenshot of the error message or problematic screen.

Sending the screenshot to Claude Code through the chat.

Asking the AI to explain what caused the error.

Receiving possible solutions and step-by-step instructions.

Applying the suggested fixes and checking whether the problem was resolved.

I used this approach because some error messages contained technical information that was difficult for me to understand without additional explanation.
